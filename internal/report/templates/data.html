2Record schema

Each line of the raw results is one JSON object. Empty fields are left out.

FieldTypeMeaning
domainstringThe scanned domain, lowercase, without www.
hoststringThe host that completed the handshake: the domain or its www. host
sectorsarraySector lists the domain is on
tranco_rankintegerRank in the Tranco list; absent for sector-only domains
ipstringIPv4 address that answered
asn, as_orginteger, stringAutonomous system of that address and its name in the iptoasn.com table
tls_versionstring1.2 or 1.3, from the default handshake
groupstringKey exchange group of the default handshake
statusstringpq-default, pq-supported, classic or unreachable
pq_groupstringThe hybrid group used, for pq-default and pq-supported
cert_issuerstringOrganisation of the certificate issuer
cert_validbooleanWhether the certificate verifies against the system roots; absent when no handshake completed
errorstringFor unreachable domains: dns, timeout, refused, tls or other
scanned_atstringTime of the scan, RFC 3339 in UTC
