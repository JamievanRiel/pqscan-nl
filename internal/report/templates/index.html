Summary

Encrypted traffic recorded today can be decrypted later if the key exchange that protected it is broken by a quantum computer. A hybrid post-quantum key exchange such as X25519MLKEM768 closes that gap, and every current browser offers one. This report measures every week which Dutch websites accept it: the .nl domains in the Tranco top 1 million and curated lists of banks, government bodies, hospitals and web shops, {{num .Scanned}} domains in total.
